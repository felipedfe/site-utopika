Euroclima | Multi-Country Coordination & Climate Policy


Regional Communication for Euroclima Program
(GIZ / European Union)
Challenge: Coordinating strategic communications across 11 Latin American countries to align national climate agendas with European Union cooperation standards.
Strategy: Management of a multidisciplinary regional hub to produce high-level technical content, managing institutional relations between international implementers and national governments.
Impact: Streamlined dissemination of climate resilience results and successful operational management of large-scale international cooperation funds.

03. utopika.com.br/project-overview/festsauva


Festival Saúva | Public Engagement, Culture & Climate Justice


Public-facing climate festival combining culture, territory,
and collective action
We conceived, designed, and produced Festival Saúva, a public-facing festival focused on climate justice, territory, and popular participation.
Our work encompassed the creation of the festival’s name and narrative, the complete visual identity, and the full production of communication materials, installations, stages, and on-site experiences.
Rooted in collective organization and grassroots movements, the festival translated complex climate debates into accessible, cultural, and participatory formats, engaging diverse audiences around transition, territory, and climate action.

04. utopika.com.br/project-overview/rosaluxredes

Rosalux | SOCIAL MEDIA STRATEGY & INSTITUTIONAL POSITIONING

Strategic social media planning for Fundação Rosa Luxemburg (Brazil)

Challenge: Strengthening the Foundation’s digital presence by transforming an already strong content production into continuous editorial engagement, narrative consistency, and increased public reach. While the organization has a solid base of content and institutional recognition, its communication lacked continuity, platform-specific adaptation, and a clearly structured narrative across channels.

Strategy: Development of a comprehensive social media strategy, including audience mapping, editorial frameworks, thematic axes, and platform-specific content approaches. The work focuses on building a consistent narrative voice, strengthening institutional protagonism, and expanding connections with key audiences through structured, continuous, and context-aware communication.

Expected Impact: Increased institutional visibility and coherence across digital platforms, stronger engagement with strategic audiences, and the consolidation of the Foundation’s social media presence as a space for critical debate, narrative dispute, and public positioning.

05. utopika.com.br/project-overview/adaptacaoclimatica

Caminhos para adaptação climática | CLIMATE ADAPTATION & URBAN RESILIENCE


Inclusive climate adaptation publication for Observatório do Clima

We developed the visual concept and editorial design for “Caminhos para um olhar inclusivo sobre adaptação climática”, a publication that connects climate adaptation, urban realities, and social justice through multiple voices and perspectives.

The project integrates design and content to translate complex debates on infrastructure, territory, and everyday life into accessible narratives, strengthening public understanding and engagement around inclusive urban resilience.
